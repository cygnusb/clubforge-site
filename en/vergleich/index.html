What about easyVerein?

easyVerein and other club-management software take care of members, fees, and finances. clubforge doesn’t replace them; it adds the communication for day-to-day club life. It takes the member list from easyVerein (or from a CSV file) and syncs it every hour, so whoever joins or leaves the club is in the app or no longer is. How that works is under Switching.

How to read this

  • clubforge fits when the club wants to keep its data in its own hands, prefers open software, and has someone who looks after a small server (themselves or through a contractor).
  • A finished service fits when nobody should look after a server and the club wants to entrust the data to the provider.
  • What else clubforge does, such as working offline (events and chats are on the device) or rides to events, is listed under Features. Events can be pulled into other calendars as a calendar subscription.
