Delete account and data

DE | EN

clubforge is a club app that each club runs on its own server. Your account belongs to your membership in that club; your data stays with the club, not with the clubforge project.

How to request deletion:

  • In the app: Profile › Delete account and data. The request goes to your club’s admins.
  • In the browser: your club’s /konto-loeschen page, for example https://app.your-club.example/konto-loeschen. It says who you can reach without the app.
  • Or directly with the club (the board or the membership office).

What happens: When you leave, access ends at once. After 30 days, your name, profile, email addresses, settings, groups, future responses and rides, and the login account are deleted. Messages stay, without your name; their content can be deleted as well if you ask. The club’s backups still contain the data for up to 30 days.

Before that, you can download your data: Profile › Download my data.
